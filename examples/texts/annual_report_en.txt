Board of Directors' report 2025 (a fictional company, written for the Vardex examples)

Demo Laks AS is a fish farming company with operations in Vestland and Nordland. The company farms Atlantic salmon at eleven sites and has 240 employees.

Operating revenue rose by 12 per cent to NOK 1,480 million, driven by higher harvest volumes and stable prices in the second half of the year. Operating profit before fair value adjustment of biomass was NOK 312 million, compared with NOK 268 million the year before. The operating margin was 21.1 per cent.

Biological challenges reduced harvest volumes in Nordland in the first quarter. Mortality from winter ulcers was higher than normal, and the company has increased its investment in fish health and preventive measures.

Tax expense was NOK 118 million, of which NOK 41 million was resource rent tax on aquaculture.

The board considers the company's financial position to be sound. Equity at year end was NOK 1,120 million, and the equity ratio was 46 per cent. The board proposes a dividend of NOK 4.50 per share.

Outlook: The company expects to harvest around 26,000 tonnes in 2026. Salmon prices are expected to remain high, but the supply situation is still uncertain.
